# ADR-0095 — GTM offer structure: free eval sample · Enterprise "Contact us" · annual updates · pricing-deferral lock

Status: accepted · 2026-06-29 (operator lock, GTM-report picker round) · extends ADR-0012 (commerce
model), ADR-0081/0082 (pricing display/posture), ADR-0089 (X-2 billing). Bundles four offer-structure
decisions from the same picker round.

## Context

The two 2026-06-29 Perplexity Computer reports + the cross-analysis
(`outputs/research/gtm-market-analysis-2026-06.md`) surfaced four offer-structure forks. The operator
resolved all four in the 2026-06-29 picker round.

## Decision

### 1. Free EU-AI-Act evaluation sample (was C3/G2)

Ship a **minimal, focused, free evaluation sample**: one clonable repo demonstrating the **EU-AI-Act
evidence path** (WORM + hash-chain + signed evidence pack walkthrough), **generated by
`create-caisson`**, under the **permissive Apache-2.0** license (it sits on the open Base, ADR-0094).
It showcases the differentiator (the evidence path) **without giving away an edition**, and serves the
funnel's evaluation step (Engaged→evaluation, ~20% in the GTM funnel). Rides the **Aug 2, 2026**
EU-AI-Act enforcement hook. Build lands in P6/GTM (work item **W3**). _Rejected:_ a broader
"Base + compliance-lite" free starter (cannibalizes the Compliance edition, blurs the paid line).

### 2. Enterprise / SLA tier — "Contact us", no price (new)

Add a founder-assisted **Enterprise / SLA tier** to the v1 SKU structure as a **"Contact us" CTA with
no public number** (pricing stays deferred). Captures high-ACV regulated-deployment buyers +
compliance agencies (market report: Enterprise $9,999–$24,999 + SLA) who will not self-serve. Stays
**true-to-built** (no fabricated price, ADR-0082) and **founder-assisted only** (consistent with the
reports' "founder-led sales, compliance only"). A small `apps/site` SKU-surface addition (work item
**W4**). _Rejected:_ out-of-scope-for-v1 (leaves the highest-ACV segment uncaptured at near-zero cost).

### 3. Update-subscription cadence — annual (supersedes monthly)

Update subscriptions renew **annually**, not monthly. A perpetual-license-plus-updates model reads as
**"regulatory insurance"** (renewal at month 10–11 in the GTM lifecycle); the monthly framing
(`$199/mo` / `$99/mo`, ADR-0081) reads like the SaaS subscription the reports explicitly say to
**avoid as core positioning**. **Supersedes the monthly cadence in ADR-0081** (numbers stay deferred —
this is cadence only). The X-2 cycle→grant mapper (ADR-0089) is therefore an **annual** cycle (work
item **W5**). _Rejected:_ keep-monthly (SaaS optics); offer-both (SKU/billing complexity, dilutes the
insurance frame).

### 4. Final pricing numbers — deferral locked deliberate (closes the standing fork)

The **exact** price numbers + grandfathering policy are **deliberately deferred to P6/checkout** — this
is now THE decision, not a perpetually-open fork. The reports' recommended **Compliance $2,999–$4,999

- ~$1,499–$1,999/yr** anchor (vs the current committed ~$1,299) and the validation tasks (20 ICP
  interviews, Ahrefs/SEMrush keyword volumes, paid WTP pilots) are **captured as the P6 repricing
  input**. The committed display (ADR-0082) stays as-is until then; any change is a re-commit +
  grandfathering decision, not a silent edit. _Rejected:_ set-a-provisional-higher-anchor-now (the
  reports' WTP is an explicit assumption pending validation; premature to move the live display).

## Binding

- A free Apache-2.0 EU-AI-Act evaluation sample (generator-emitted) is in scope for P6/GTM (W3).
- The v1 SKU structure includes a no-price "Contact us" Enterprise/SLA tier (W4).
- Update subscriptions are **annual**; the X-2 mapper (ADR-0089) is an annual cycle (W5). The monthly
  cadence in ADR-0081 is superseded.
- Exact pricing numbers + grandfathering are deferred to P6/checkout, with the reports' anchor +
  validation tasks recorded as the input. The pricing fork is **closed** (deliberately deferred), not
  open.

Evidence: `outputs/research/gtm-market-analysis-2026-06.md` §2 + §4 (G1/G4) + Fork register; the
operator picker rounds (2026-06-29).
