## 2. Current-state read (`page.tsx` + tokens)

- **Hero** (`page.tsx:61-111`): single-column, left-aligned. Eyebrow (keyword) → H1 "Fail-closed by construction." (display clamp 2.75–5.5rem, Hubot Sans, tracking-tighter) → lede (feature list, before/after frame) → primary copper… **actually teal** CTA "Request early access" + ghost "Read the docs" → a bare `<pre className="cs-code">` psql RLS-denial block (max 60ch), lots of empty space below.
- **Accent is cold-steel teal**, not copper: `--cs-accent: oklch(0.74 0.115 205)` (`tokens.css:14`), on cool near-black `--cs-bg: oklch(0.16 0.012 220)`. Status tokens already exist: `--cs-danger` red 25, `--cs-success` green 150, `--cs-warning` amber 75 (`tokens.css:21-23`).
- **Type:** Hubot Sans (body weight **350** — light), Martian Mono. Radii 4/8/12/16/pill.
- **Section rhythm** (`global.css:96-103`): every section = `padding-block: space-24` + `border-top: 1px hairline`. Uniform. Hero is `--flush` (no top border).
- **Sections:** Umbrella/named-enemy (text only) → "What ships in the box" 3-card grid (unicode glyphs ▣▤▥ + label + body) → Editions 2-col link-cards (Hero accented) → SKU structure 4-col (no prices, ADR-0048) → Waitlist.
- **Nav** (`site-nav.tsx`): sticky, backdrop-blur, hairline border-bottom, `caisson` mono wordmark + links + theme toggle. **No persistent CTA button.**
- **What's already strong:** the evidence-over-adjectives instinct (real RLS denial in the hero), the named enemy (happy-path boilerplate), the honest no-price SKU surface, the before/after lede. The craft gap is _treatment_, not _concept_.

## 3. Key findings

1. **Caisson's "code-in-hero" is more differentiated than the references, not less.** Resend, HTTPie, Jasper all push code to a _proof section_ below an editorial/abstract hero. Caisson leading the fold with a real fail-closed denial is a stronger evidence-over-adjectives play — _provided the artifact is crafted like a real terminal, not a bare `<pre>`._ The fork is treatment + pairing, not "keep vs cut."
2. **The dark-devtools sea is cool-accented** (Supabase green, Checkly blue, Warp violet, Depot green, Caisson's teal-205). The strongest _differentiated_ analogs — Cron, Pipe, Svelte, Mode — all use a **single warm accent** (orange/molten/copper) on near-black. A warm copper would both stand out in the category **and** snap Caisson back onto the gridwork-core copper floor (`#E8923A`). This is the single highest-leverage brand fork.
3. **Linear's security page is a ready-made blueprint** for every evidence surface Caisson needs: debossed cert _medallions_ over colored badges, and a _left-intro / right-dense-grid_ section rhythm that reads as engineering spec-sheet, not marketing.
4. **Pre-launch, Caisson has no logos or testimonials to show** — so it must substitute _proof of rigor for proof of adoption_: real test names, CI green checks, the standards-gate, a hash-chain snippet. "Cross-tenant isolation is a test, not a hope" (already in copy) wants to become a _visible artifact_.
5. **Image-light is both on-brand and an SEO/LCP win.** Langbase's "avoid hero imagery, prefer code-native visualizations" + ADR-0045 static export / unoptimized images / Plausible → the code/config artifacts ARE the imagery. A heavy 3D hero object (Resend cube) would tank LCP and read as slop here.
6. **Depth discipline is already correct** (`global.css:3` "depth = tonal surface + hairline borders, never shadows") and matches every top reference (Warp, Linear, Depot). Keep it; the references just push it further with _alternating surface bands_ for rhythm.

## 5. Steal-list (concrete tactics, with source)

1. **Terminal-window chrome with a status chip** (not a bare `<pre>`): a prompt/filename bar + a `● RLS fail-closed` chip in `--cs-success`; tint the `ERROR:` line in `--cs-danger`; underline "fail-closed" in the accent. — warp.dev `40a9c295`, resend.com editor, tokens.css:21-23.
2. **Left-intro / right-dense-grid section rhythm** for compliance controls: a bold lead sentence + muted continuation on the left, a 2-col labeled feature grid with tiny monoline icons on the right. — linear.app/security `7035f422`.
3. **Debossed cert medallions** (SOC 2 / HIPAA / GDPR / EU AI Act) rendered as dark embossed coins, monochrome — framed as "evidence packs you generate," not "we are certified." — linear.app `7035f422`.
4. **A proof artifact inside every evidence card**: a single mono line that _is_ the proof (`FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, `ObjectLockMode: COMPLIANCE`, a SHA-256 chain link). — linear controls grid + Caisson's own evidence-over-adjectives instinct.
5. **Single warm accent on near-black** as the category-differentiating brand move (copper/amber vs the teal/green/blue sea). — cron.com `0528b40d`, pipe.com `c00d3961`.
6. **Alternating surface bands** (`--cs-bg` ↔ `--cs-surface-1`) to give the named-enemy beat narrative weight instead of a uniform hairline. — depot.dev `707c2922`.
7. **Persistent nav CTA** ("Request early access") + transparent-over-hero nav that gains a hairline + bg on scroll. — resend.com, linear.app, warp.dev.
8. **Proof-of-rigor over proof-of-adoption**: render a real cross-tenant isolation _test_ (`expect(otherTenantRows).toHaveLength(0)` ✓) where a logo wall would normally sit — Caisson is pre-launch and has no logos. — heuristic + page.tsx:18.
9. **Mono-display H1 at 60–72px** as an alternative to Hubot Sans for the hero — reinforces the watertight/engineering identity and the monospace-adjacent wordmark. — langbase.com `6faf7a27`, ADR-0041.
10. **Image-light is the honest + fast choice**: code/config artifacts as the only "imagery"; keeps LCP sub-second under the static-export constraint. — langbase rule, ADR-0045.

## 6. Recommended hero direction (synthesis)

A **split hero on dark**: left column = keyword eyebrow → mono-or-Hubot H1 "Fail-closed by construction." → before/after lede → primary "Request early access" + ghost "Read the docs" → a one-line mono credential strip. Right column = a **framed terminal artifact** (chrome + `● RLS fail-closed` chip) showing the real psql denial, `ERROR:` tinted danger, "fail-closed" underlined in the accent. Background flat near-black with an optional faint engineering grid or single waterline hairline. Below: an alternating-band named-enemy beat → a Linear-style left-intro/right-grid "what ships" controls section with a proof artifact per item → debossed cert medallions framed as _generated evidence_ → launch-sequence editions → no-price SKU surface → waitlist. The two brand-defining choices to put to the operator individually and first: **B1 (warm copper accent vs teal)** and **B2 (mono-display vs Hubot H1)** — everything else composes cleanly on top of whichever way those land.
