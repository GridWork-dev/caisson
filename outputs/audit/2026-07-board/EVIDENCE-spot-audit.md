One staleness flag: "site is CF-Access-gated" is no longer observably true, which softens the "traffic zeroes are purely pre-launch" framing in E-P1/E-D22. The most stretch sits in the **first-party section's §5 lock table** (the one place the pack asserts something now-false as binding) and, structurally, in the **competitor section's carried-forward items (E-C12)** — correctly disclosed as unverified, but eight baseline rows resting on no current observation. The market-voice and competitor items that were re-fetched are the pack's strongest material: every one reproduced against the live web.
