## 4. Minority Report (verbatim)

Per both critiques' explicit instruction not to average away the seller-plane disagreement,
both verdicts are preserved word-for-word.

**T1 (majority position in this domain — 1 of 3 TECH seats, but the more actionable claim):**

> "The seller plane is deliberate reckless debt at this stage: a bespoke Python support bot,
> standalone RAG service, and compliance/intel daemon add a second toolchain and multiple
> deploy, credential, and runbook surfaces before the product has more than one observed
> visitor or any activation signal."
>
> Recommendation: "...scale `caisson-docs` plus `caisson-support-bot` to zero after preserving
> static `llms.txt` and a health-safe unavailable state; this explicitly requires superseding
> ADR-0009, ADR-0096, ADR-0105, and ADR-0206 rather than pretending those custom always-live
> support-stack locks do not exist, while ADR-0286/ADR-0369's existing local intel daemon gets
> maintenance only and no new scope."

**T4 (dissent — the seat that did the file-level re-verification pass):**

> "The only true cross-cutting outliers are two Python islands — `services/support-bot`
> (~7k LOC plus a substantial tests/evals suite) and a second `pyproject` home under `tools/`
> ... inside a TypeScript-strict monorepo: deliberate and deployed, but they double the
> language toolchain a solo operator must keep fluent, so the rule is keep both and freeze
> Python at exactly two."
>
> "...keep both Python islands frozen at two (kill-cost: rewriting ~7k LOC plus an eval suite
> to save one toolchain)."

**Claude critique's framing of this exact conflict (verbatim, preserved as the adjudication
note — not a new claim, a quote of the existing critique):**

> "T1 broke that spell (supersede four ADRs by name); T4 did not. Preserve both verdicts side
> by side — their disagreement about the support bot (T1: scale to zero; T4: keep, frozen at
> two islands) is real dissent the ledger must not average away."

No disposition is offered here beyond preservation — resolving kill-vs-keep on the seller
plane requires either the ADR-0009/0096/0105/0206 supersession T1 names (untested by any
other seat) or T4's proposed dead-surface sweep; neither has run.
