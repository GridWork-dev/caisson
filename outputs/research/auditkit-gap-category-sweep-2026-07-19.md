## 0. What CAISSON-76 already established (not repeated here)

The prior round built a full feature-parity table against **AuditKit.dev** specifically (hybrid
hosted audit-log SDK + SOC2-prep GRC-lite, AGPLv3 core + commercial `/ee`, $99–999+/mo) and
produced: 11 "caisson-ahead" claims (external WORM anchor, field-crypto + crypto-shred, OSCAL
export, structural claim-honesty via the `CrosswalkReference` type, RFC-3161 timestamping, etc.),
4 build-candidates (**access-review campaigns, general risk register, policy-template content
pack, SIEM channel drivers**), 2 wrong-class verdicts (**auditor collaboration portal, trust
center/personnel tracker** — hosted-SaaS shapes foreign to an owned-source library), and 2 watch
items (**vendor tracking** — CRUD-shaped, low differentiation; **AI anomaly detection** —
philosophically in tension with the deterministic "flag-never-guess" evidence posture). That
table stays the source of truth for AuditKit specifically; this round's job is the wider category
and closing the backlog line at `docs/state/outstanding-work.md:116` into a SPEC-ready shape.

## 2. Gap analysis vs Caisson's shipped surface

Shipped compliance-relevant packages (per `docs/build-state.md`, `packages/*/README.md`):
`audit-worm` (WORM chain + external anchor), `compliance` + `compliance-core` (deterministic
flag-never-guess collectors + OSCAL export), `frameworks-pack` (SOC2/HIPAA/EU-AI-Act +
ISO-27001 crosswalk + cross-framework rollup, PR#238/ADR-0333), `signing-primitive`,
`field-crypto`, `tenancy-rls`, `retention-runner` (proves the scheduled-task-riding-`@caisson/jobs`
reuse pattern), `alerting` (`AlertChannel` port: email/webhook/Slack/Telegram), `org-controls`.
**Verified by grep: zero SBOM handling anywhere in the repo** — any SBOM-shaped candidate is
greenfield, not composable.

### Explicit non-fits (with reasons)

1. **Multi-cloud infra scanning as a first-class product** — real demand, wrong shape for a library that sells composable evidence primitives, not a fleet-scanning service.
2. **Hosted auditor/reviewer portals** — every instance in this sweep that ships one is a SaaS company; Caisson's honest substitute stays the downloadable signed evidence pack.
3. **LLM-based "AI anomaly detection" or evidence-guessing** — structurally opposed to the flag-never-guess discipline that is Caisson's actual differentiator against this entire category.
4. **Vendor CRM (plain CRUD vendor list + reminders)** — no technical-control content; only reconsider under the SBOM-evidence reframe above.
