## 4. Limitations — what could not be verified

- **SEO-content-mill pattern in Segment B.** Many 2026 "HIPAA-compliant Next.js agency" search results share near-identical phrasing across unrelated domains ("Server Components keep PHI off the client," matching FAQ blocks, matching pricing-tier language) — a sign of templated/programmatic content rather than distinct businesses. Rows 17-18 (ZTABS, Branch Boston) were flagged low-medium confidence specifically for this reason and lack a named individual; every Segment B row should get a human gut-check call before any partner ask lands.
- **TypeScript-specifically was not confirmed** (vs. general React/Node/Python) for rows 5, 7, 9, 10, 20 — job postings and marketing copy often list "React" or "modern web stack" without naming the language; flagged medium/low confidence accordingly rather than assumed.
- **Several open-source candidates are very early** (2-9 GitHub stars, solo maintainer): rows 21, 24, 25, 27-30. They are real, verifiable, and precisely on-thesis technically, but are individual-maintainer leads, not committed budget-holders at an established company — worth a different conversation (technical collaboration / integration) than the 40%-discount purchase-based program, unless the maintainer is also the founder of a company that would buy.
- **No private data collected.** Every contact listed is a channel the organization itself publishes (a YC launch-post "founders@…" ask, a company contact-us page, a public GitHub profile, an npm maintainer email, a public LinkedIn profile URL). No scraping of private data, no unlisted personal emails, no phone numbers beyond what a business publishes on its own site.
- **Recency decays fast at seed stage.** Funding amounts, headcounts, and "actively hiring" signals are only as fresh as each row's cited crawl/publish date (mostly 2025-07 through 2026-07). Reconfirm before outreach — seed-stage facts shift in weeks, not months.
- **Some hits were dropped per the row-drop rule.** At least one job-board listing described a real "seed-stage healthtech AI platform" with a TypeScript-specific requirement but did not disclose the company's name in the accessible listing — dropped rather than guessed at.
- **Community channels are not one-shot.** FINOS Slack, Probo's Discord, and the Fintech Compliance Circle require joining and participating over time before any partner ask reads as credible rather than as spam — flagged explicitly in §3, not just implied.
