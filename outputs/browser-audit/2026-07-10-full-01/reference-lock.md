## Brief

Auditing Caisson's production marketing, buyer, and admin web surfaces for technical founders and operators evaluating or using compliance-grade infrastructure. The primary goals are evidence-led purchase confidence, transparent account state, and dependable operational control. The tone is engineered, exact, and load-bearing. The main risk is a polished surface that hides missing evidence, ambiguous commercial state, inaccessible controls, or unsafe behavior. Research covers styles, screens, and flows; the path is audit-only.
