    uid=37_36 StaticText "WORM live proofs"
    uid=37_37 StaticText "A live-verification harness runs against a real S3 Object-Lock bucket and asserts the guarantee end to end: a write succeeds and reads back a real retention date, a duplicate write is refused with a real HTTP 412 (write-once), a retention extension lands a later date, and an attempt to shorten it is refused while the lock still holds. It runs against real cloud storage — not a mock."

These are the working security records behind the code, available to a reviewer under license."
