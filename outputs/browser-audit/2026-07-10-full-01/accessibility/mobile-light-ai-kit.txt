    uid=32_24 heading "One package, four other Caisson packages behind it." level="2"
    uid=32_25 StaticText "The kit is one package, @caisson/ai-kit, wired around four other Caisson packages: prompt-registry resolves and renders the versioned prompt, ai-meter reserves against the tenant's cap before the call and reconciles the real usage after, guardrails runs the input and output through a Zod-typed schema and policy check, and ai-config maps the call's lane to a provider.
