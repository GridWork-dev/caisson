# Three-ring journeys

## Ring 1 — public

Prove discovery-to-decision journeys across home, bundle pages, marketplace/plans/module detail, docs/search, comparisons, stack fit, evidence/security/procurement, glossary, legal, login entry, cart discovery, responsive navigation, and external handoff boundaries.

## Ring 2 — buyer probe

Prove login and recovery, dashboard orientation, plan/subscription state, license/update window, credits, invoices, activity, members, AI keys, compliance, and cart. Mutate only the dedicated probe account and only with a complete compensator. Do not buy, irreversibly cancel, or change identity/authentication.

## Ring 3 — authorized admin

Prove cockpit navigation and role boundary across overview, architecture, decisions, ops/logs, business/commerce/audit, catalog surfaces, and intel triage. Exercise loading, empty, denied, error, and recovery states. Read-only is default. Mutations require an operator-allowlisted synthetic fixture and a verified compensator.

## Boundaries per journey

Cover signed-out/signed-in, entitled/unentitled, empty/populated, desktop/mobile, light/dark, keyboard/pointer, reduced-motion/default, success/failure, and slow/degraded dependencies only when safely reproducible.
