## Your license is perpetual

A one-time purchase is a **perpetual license**: the software keeps working forever, verified
offline with an Ed25519 signature, no phone-home, no server we can take down or you can lose
access to. The license is valid for the major version you purchased.

## The updates window

Every purchase includes a **12-month updates window** starting at purchase. During the window,
pull any version published to the registry within that window, bug fixes, new modules, minor
releases, all of it.

**Expiry is never punitive.** When the window closes, nothing is revoked: every version you
already pulled keeps installing forever, and so does anything that was published inside your
window, even if you pull it after the window closes. An expired window just stops new releases
from counting, it does not touch what you already have.

## Windows are per-entitlement

An updates window belongs to the entitlement it was purchased with, not to your account as a
whole, a bundle purchase and an à-la-carte module purchase each carry their own window. If you
own the same module both à la carte and through a bundle, the more favorable of the two windows
applies.

## Subscriptions work differently

If you're on a subscription rather than a one-time purchase, updates are included the whole time
the subscription is active, there's no window to track and nothing to renew separately.
