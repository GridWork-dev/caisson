### Control rules

1. A platform approval does not settle legal ownership, tax treatment, employment status, or IP title.
2. A family intention is not reported as a completed transfer, ownership interest, wage, reimbursement, contribution, or loan.
3. Liam is not described as a current member or current beneficial owner unless counsel reaches and documents that conclusion.
4. The mother's legal ownership is reported truthfully on applications unless counsel implements a different structure and the platform accepts updated information.
5. No one uses another person's banking, Paddle, tax, password-manager, or administrator credentials.
6. Connected financial data is analyzed read-only. No transfer, payment, account edit, or permanent recategorization is authorized by this project.
7. This map changes only when a source record, signed decision, platform result, or advisor conclusion changes.

### Directions counsel should compare

1. **Parent owned until 18.** Lakshmi is the genuine legal and economic owner until August 19, 2027. Liam works as a properly documented employee or agent. Wages, reimbursements, and personal gifts are kept separate. A binding transfer mechanism moves and admits the entire interest at 18.
2. **Present beneficial-interest structure.** A trust or other enforceable arrangement recognizes Liam's current economic interest while preserving lawful adult control. Counsel defines ownership, fiduciary duties, tax reporting, KYC disclosures, distributions, and the age-18 conversion.
3. **Corrective restructuring before revenue.** If the current records do not match the family's actual legal and economic intent, counsel restructures before live revenue so title, tax filings, platform disclosures, and cash flows tell the same story.

The choice must be real, not just drafting language. It affects who owns profits, who bears tax, who may receive distributions, who reports as an owner or controller, who employs Liam, and what Mercury and Paddle must be told.

### Automatic-transfer design requirements

The final structure should, if counsel confirms it is lawful and effective:

- cause the 100% interest to transfer on August 19, 2027 without requiring a discretionary future decision;
- admit Liam as the sole voting member and continue the company's management and contracts;
- define any consideration, valuation, gift reporting, consent, notice, and ledger entry;
- state what happens if Lakshmi or Liam dies or becomes incapacitated before the trigger;
- prevent a gap in authorized banking, Paddle, code-signing, or customer-support control;
- coordinate the operating agreement with any trust or transfer instrument;
- include any confirmation or ratification Liam should sign after reaching majority; and
- require prompt KYC, tax, bank, and platform updates after the transfer.

Do not use a standard custodial arrangement merely because it is familiar if its mandatory termination age or control rules cannot deliver the fixed age-18 outcome.
