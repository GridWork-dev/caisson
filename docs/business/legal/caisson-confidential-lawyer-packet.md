The father currently provides Liam approximately $200 per week to the Current account, along with some larger deposits described as gifts. Those family transfers are not company payroll in the records reviewed. Any future wages should be paid and recorded through a deliberate company payroll process rather than relabeling personal transfers after the fact without advisor support.

Current Georgia Department of Labor guidance states that work permits are not required after a minor reaches 16 and that 16- and 17-year-olds have no state or federal work-hour restrictions. It separately preserves hazardous/prohibited-occupation limits for minors and lists a parent-owned-business exception. Counsel should confirm how those rules apply to a mother-owned single-member LLC, the exact duties, and any school, wage, recordkeeping, insurance, or platform conditions; the packet does not treat the general guidance as a complete employment analysis.
