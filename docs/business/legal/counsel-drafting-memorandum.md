2. Lakshmi is settlor and initial trustee. Legal title is held by Lakshmi solely as trustee, and the trustee is admitted as Caisson's sole member.

4. Lakshmi separately serves as initial LLC manager. Her manager office is not an economic interest.

This design gives Liam present profits and appreciation after closing while preserving adult legal control. The structure is intended to create a present completed gift, but an irrevocable label or state-law vesting description does not decide that federal tax result.

## 2. Recommended architecture and rejected alternatives

### 2.1 Why a present full-member trust

Liam must receive the present benefit of profits and appreciation, not merely a later gift. The trust should therefore receive the entire LLC interest now. All distributions, appreciation, sale proceeds, and replacement property attributable to that interest are held for Liam, subject to company reserves and the pre-handoff death condition.

Georgia separates economic assignment from member admission. An assignee ordinarily receives economics without voting or management rights, and the assignor remains a member until admission occurs. See O.C.G.A. Sections 14-11-502, 14-11-503, and 14-11-505; Hopson v. Bank of North Georgia, 258 Ga. App. 360, 574 S.E.2d 411 (2002).

An economic-only trust would leave Lakshmi personally as member while the trust held value, complicating succession and KYC. The cleaner structure records the serving trustee in fiduciary capacity as sole member, Lakshmi individually as manager, and Liam as beneficiary.

### 2.2 Why a future-transfer promise is insufficient

A promise to give the LLC in 2027 would not give Liam present economics and would remain exposed to death, incapacity, creditor, delivery, and enforceability risks. Georgia gift law generally requires intent, acceptance, and delivery; equity generally does not specifically enforce a voluntary promise. See O.C.G.A. Sections 44-5-80 through 44-5-83 and 23-2-132.

A private express trust must be signed, identify its property, beneficiary, trustee, purpose, and duties, and actually receive legal title. See O.C.G.A. Sections 53-12-20 and 53-12-25. It should reserve no broad revocation power. See O.C.G.A. Section 53-12-40.

## 4. Trust drafting terms

### 4.1 Property and beneficial interest

The trust is an irrevocable Georgia private express trust. Its property is the entire Caisson interest and every distribution, accretion, substitution, claim, and proceed attributable to it. The trustee, not an abstract trust name, holds legal title in fiduciary capacity.

For state-law drafting purposes, while Liam is living before the handoff he is intended to hold the sole current beneficial interest, intended to be vested subject to administration, a spendthrift restriction, and complete divestment only if he dies before the handoff. Counsel must confirm that characterization. It does not determine income-tax ownership, gift-tax present-interest status, platform beneficial ownership, or direct LLC membership. Liam has no pre-handoff right to withdraw trust property, direct a company vote, remove the trustee, use fiduciary credentials, or bind Caisson merely because he is beneficiary.

Company distributions are paid to the trustee and recorded in a separate trust ledger and account. The trust may pay taxes legally imposed on it. Any support payment, tax distribution, or reimbursement to Lakshmi, Liam, or another deemed tax owner requires written counsel and tax advice identifying the legal obligation, taxpayer, payee, formula, cap, and gift or estate consequences. Wages, reimbursements, parental gifts, and trust distributions must use separate approvals and records.

### 4.2 Irrevocability and fiduciary controls

No settlor, trustee, manager, protector, or advisor may revoke the trust, add a beneficiary, redirect Liam's economics, postpone the handoff, alter the death condition, or use trust property for a personal obligation. Narrow administrative correction may be allowed only to comply with law or preserve the fixed result, with independent or court oversight. See O.C.G.A. Sections 53-12-61, 53-12-240, and 53-12-246.

Lakshmi will be settlor, trustee, and manager. The instruments should require capacity labels, separate books and accounts, fair terms and independent review for related-party transactions, documented compensation and reimbursements, periodic reporting, and beneficiary-first treatment of trustee decisions. Ordinary manager decisions may use a good-faith business-judgment standard, but trustee discretion cannot divert value.

The trustee may retain, finance, restructure, or sell Caisson in good faith. Owner-level cash, securities, rollover equity, notes, earnouts, escrow releases, indemnity recoveries, and other replacement value remain trust property. Company-level sale proceeds remain company property until lawfully distributed.

The trustee may vote the LLC interest, make capital contributions, approve a bona fide financing, retain concentrated ownership, settle owner-level claims, and engage advisors, but only for a documented trust or company purpose. A pledge of the LLC interest should require commercially reasonable terms, written risk analysis, and the independent approval mechanism selected by counsel. No borrowing may fund a personal obligation or related-party benefit. A sale or recapitalization should carry the fixed economics into every replacement security and contingent payment, with schedules for escrowed claims, holdbacks, earnouts, indemnity recoveries, and later receipts. The trustee may retain a reasonable tax and expense reserve, but the trust instrument should state the cap, release deadline, reporting, and who receives later recoveries.

### 4.3 Death and mandatory distribution

If Liam dies before 12:01 a.m. Eastern Time on August 19, 2027, his beneficial interest ends and the trust property shifts to Lakshmi, subject to final liabilities. Counsel must supply the remainder if Lakshmi is not then living or order of death is uncertain.

If Liam is living, all beneficial and assignable trust property vests in him at the handoff. LLC membership and governance rights become his only upon valid adult consent and admission. A reasonable final tax reserve may remain in bare administration for Liam, with a cap, deadline, and accounting.

Reserved matters should include equity, member admission, amendment of protected provisions, merger, dissolution, substantially all assets, core-IP transfer, material debt or guaranty, related-party transactions, material tax elections, and changes to bank, payment, domain, repository, or signing-key control.

He may not use another person's credentials or sign an adult-only, member, manager, bank, KYC, equity, debt, settlement, tax, or core-IP act. Titles must not imply membership.

The company should maintain the amended articles, operating agreement, trust certification, assignment, member and manager admissions, ledgers, authority matrix, succession documents, and escrow instructions. Third parties should receive a limited trust certification where sufficient, not the full private instrument.

Trustee succession and LLC admission should be separate steps even when they occur in one closing. On a transition, legal title passes to the accepted successor trustee under the trust documents; the successor also must deliver written consent to LLC membership and the operating agreement, satisfy the advance admission conditions, and be entered on the member ledger. The change should be described as no change in beneficiary or percentage, not as no change in legal owner. Likewise, successor-manager acceptance requires its own document. A person may accept one office and decline the other, in which case the professional fallback fills only the vacant office.

### 6.2 Majority and statutory consent

Georgia sets majority at 18. O.C.G.A. Section 39-1-1. Georgia precedent applies a day-before-birthday rule in some statutory contexts. In re A.P.S., 304 Ga. App. 193, 696 S.E.2d 483 (2010). Do not infer contractual capacity on August 18 from those cases alone. Unless counsel gives a written opinion supporting an earlier time, Liam should execute adult joinders no earlier than August 19, 2027, while the documents separately preserve the intended 12:01 a.m. economic trigger to the maximum lawful extent.

An assignee cannot be admitted until consenting, and a minor's contract is generally voidable. O.C.G.A. Sections 14-11-505 and 13-3-20. Adult consent is substantive, not a ministerial defect that can be deemed satisfied.

If Liam's valid adult joinder is not in escrow at the handoff, every independently effective economic-distribution step should occur, but Liam must not be deemed admitted or appointed manager. The trustee remains record member solely pending lawful admission, and the serving manager continues under a limited continuity mandate. Neither may redirect Liam's economics, issue equity, postpone a valid admission, or exercise broader discretion than preservation requires. Counsel should provide expedited-court and cure procedures.

The closing package should contain the trust distribution and assignment, advance member consent, Liam's adult joinder, conditional admission, conditional manager transition, ledgers, bank and platform resolutions, tax instructions, and an evidence checklist. Assignment transfers economics; governance rights arise only on admission.

The escrow should be staged. The present adult parties deposit irrevocable assignments, consents, manager actions, and company records after the trust closing. Liam receives independent advice and deposits his adult joinder during the counsel-confirmed majority window. The escrow holder verifies identity, signatures, document hashes, the continued existence of the LLC interest or any substitute property, and evidence that Liam is living. At release, the holder records the exact Eastern Time, documents released, resulting member and manager ledgers, property distributed, reserves retained, and each platform update submitted. A 60-to-90-day pre-closing review should confirm majority-date law, tax and valuation updates, successor availability, and recovery access for bank, domain, repository, email, password manager, cloud, and signing keys. Post-closing updates should be assigned to named owners and completed within fixed business-day deadlines.

## 7. Death, incapacity, and succession

Upon Lakshmi's death, incapacity, resignation, removal, or inability to serve, Vik Thadani becomes trustee only after written acceptance. He becomes LLC member in fiduciary capacity only after satisfying the preapproved admission conditions, including consent to the operating agreement and delivery of a trust certification. Legal title changes trustees; Liam's beneficial ownership and percentage do not change.

Vik Thadani separately accepts the manager office. Acceptance of one office does not confer the other. A nominee has no emergency authority merely from designation; preservation authority begins only through a valid acceptance, temporary appointment, or court order.

If Vik Thadani cannot serve, an independent professional fiduciary becomes trustee under an objective appointment mechanism, and a qualified professional or court-supervised person becomes manager. The fallback must preserve bank funds, payroll, customer support, repositories, domains, cloud services, signing keys, tax filings, and the fixed handoff without changing economics.

Georgia default rules can end individual membership on death or adjudicated incompetence and create last-member dissolution risk. See O.C.G.A. Sections 14-11-506, 14-11-601.1, and 14-11-602. The definitive documents should override avoidable gaps and state that Caisson continues perpetually.

Funding the trust is intended as a present transfer of valuable property.
