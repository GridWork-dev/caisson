## What this is — and isn't

This is an **evaluation sample**, not the product. It shows the evidence-path _pattern_ a regulated
AI deployment under the EU AI Act (Art. 12 record-keeping; enforcement from 2 Aug 2026) needs to
reason about. It does **not** include:

- a WORM (write-once) evidence store,
- the full versioned evidence-pack generator,
- the compliance control catalogue / framework mapping.

Those ship in the commercial **Compliance edition** — see https://caisson.sh/compliance.

## How it works

`src/evidence-path.ts` walks four steps:

1. **APPEND** — three illustrative evidence records for one automated decision (invoke → render →
   human review).
2. **CHAIN** — fold them into a SHA-256 hash chain via `@caisson/kernel`'s `buildChain`/`anchorChain`
   (tamper-evident: altering, reordering, or dropping an interior record breaks every hash after it).
3. **SIGN** — a detached Ed25519 signature (`node:crypto`) over the chain's anchor, binding it to a
   key holder.
4. **VERIFY** — recompute the chain and re-check the signature; both must pass.
