The dated-commentary registry now has a drafting lane that is separate from the published one. A drafted piece is held in its own list that the hub, the spoke routes, the lookup helper, the sitemap, and the source watch all ignore, so a draft cannot reach the live site because someone forgot a filter; publishing is moving the record into the published list. The first draft is a piece stating legal conclusions about a live regulation, which the operator publishes after reviewing those conclusions.
